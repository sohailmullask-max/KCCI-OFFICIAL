THE BEGINNING

KCCI HISTORY

From a local cricket initiative to a structured grassroots cricket institution. This is the story of KCCI and its continuing journey.

KCCI, also known as Kabristan Cricket Control Institute, is a grassroots cricket institution based in Kathor, Surat, Gujarat. KCCI is dedicated to the development, organization, and promotion of competitive cricket at the grassroots level.


The journey of KCCI began in 2019 as a local cricket initiative. Over the years, the organization developed its playing standards, administration, match structure, equipment, and overall cricketing system. In 2026, KCCI was officially established as a structured and professionally managed cricket board.

KCCI has created a platform where players can develop their batting, bowling, wicketkeeping, fielding and leadership skills.

THE JOURNEY

IMPORTANT MILESTONES

Key moments that shaped the development of KCCI.

2019 • THE BEGINNING

The journey of KCCI began as a local cricket initiative in Kathor.

PLASTIC STUMPS

Plastic stumps were introduced, replacing the old wooden or plywood setup used during the early days.

PROPER TROPHIES

Proper fibre trophies were introduced, bringing a more organized competitive experience to KCCI.

FORMAT REVOLUTION

Test, ODI and T20 formats were introduced, along with structured series between teams.

EQUIPMENT DEVELOPMENT

Proper packets of cricket balls, bats and better cricket equipment became part of the developing system.

2026 • OFFICIAL ESTABLISHMENT

KCCI was officially established as a structured and professionally managed cricket board.

THE STORY BEHIND THE GAME

FROM LOCAL TO ORGANIZED CRICKET

In the early days, KCCI basically had only one format of cricket. It was an unlimited-over format, mostly played with fast or aggressive bowling. There was no proper division of formats and there were many unusual rules and regulations.


The systems were gradually changed and proper cricket formats were introduced. KCCI brought three major formats: Test, ODI and T20. The concept of series and structured matches between teams was also introduced.


The development continued with better equipment, proper cricket balls, bats, rules and team structures. Tournaments were eventually introduced as part of the developing cricket system.

THE FUTURE

VISION & MISSION

KCCI'S VISION

To develop KCCI into a leading grassroots cricket institution, provide players with a professional and competitive environment, establish high standards of cricket administration, and take organized plastic-ball cricket from the local level to the national stage.

KCCI'S MISSION

To organize structured cricket, develop local talent, maintain professional standards, promote discipline and fair competition, and create a sustainable cricketing platform where players can develop their abilities.

THE BIGGER DREAM

FROM KATHOR TO THE NATIONAL STAGE

One of KCCI's major visions is to take plastic-ball cricket from the grassroots level to the national level.


KCCI aims to develop standardized formats, rules, player selection systems, competitions and professional administration for plastic-ball cricket, with the long-term goal of expanding its recognition across India.
